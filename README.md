# Super Azubi

An HTML5 jump'n'run. You play an IT trainee. Level 1: cross the hotel lobby to reach the
elevator – over luggage, PCs, network cables, switches and phones, past colleagues who all
have "just a quick question", while your own phone keeps ringing. At the end waits
Mr. Pfefferkorn, who forgets his password every five minutes. Level 2: the 4th-floor
open-plan office – get to the IT storage room past desks, printers and even more colleagues.
Level 3: the attic server room – reach the core switch past racks, UPS boxes and the network crew.

**Play:** https://salihzett.github.io/super-azubi/ – or open `index.html` in a browser.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| ← → / A D | Move |
| Space / ↑ / W | Jump (hold for higher) |
| V | Talk nonsense – confuses the boss for a few seconds |
| M | Sound on/off |
| ESC | Back to title |
| L | Switch language (title screen) |

On phones: on-screen buttons.

## Goal

- Jump on colleagues to close their tickets. Touching them costs a life.
- Gold coins = points, chocolate bar = extra life.
- Don't fall into the fountain.
- Boss: jump on his head 5 times. Press V when he gets annoying.
- Reach the elevator (level 1), the IT storage room (level 2) and the core switch (level 3). Faster = time bonus. Enter your name for the leaderboard.
